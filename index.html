How the numbers are produced

Profit per item = (sell price × quality multiplier − setup fee − sales fee) − (material cost + station fee) + returned resource value. Materials are priced at the cheapest city that has a fresh enough quote; the product is priced at the best city including the Black Market.

Refining recipes are exact. Equipment and cooking recipes use per-slot templates — accurate in shape, and every one can be overridden per item id via recipeOverrides in your saved settings. Station fees are modelled as silver-per-100-item-value, which is how the in-game station tax actually behaves.

Price data from the community-run Albion Online Data Project. Quotes are uploaded by players, so thin markets can be stale or missing entirely.
